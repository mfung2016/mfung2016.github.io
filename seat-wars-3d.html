SEAT WARS 3D

智華館搶位大戰

Stake out a seat in the Learning Commons or the Main Library and hold it — 90 seconds on the clock. The red crowd will try to take it off you.

HOW SCORING WORKS
points per second = seat value × battery × hold bonus
  • Points tick every second you hold a seat, and stop the instant you stand up or someone takes it.
  • Battery above 50% = DOUBLE points. Below that you earn single. Chargers are marked with a small ⚡ bolt floating above them — hold E for 1.2s to get +30.
  • Hold longer, earn faster. The bonus climbs up to ×3 over 15 seconds of sitting still.
  • Seat values: ×1, ×1.25, ×1.5. Press Q to leave your bag on one seat and go sit somewhere else — two seats at once is the max.
  • Someone already sitting? Hold E to challenge — it's rock-paper-scissors.
